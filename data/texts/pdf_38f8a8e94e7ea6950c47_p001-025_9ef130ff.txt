INTRODUCTION 
 
The Year is 1491 DR. Times on the Swordcoast of 
Faerun are prosperous. The grand cities of the 
Lords’ Alliance are growing, and trade is increasing. 
Unfortunately, this led to disputes and a falling out 
with the egalitarian and ecological factions, the 
Harper and the Emerald Enclave.  
In the shadows, another faction also profits from the 
rise of the cities. The Zhentarim, its mercenary 
monopoly, and their more dubious lines of business 
flourish. Pereghost, the leader of the black network, 
seizes the opportunity. He plans to rebuild their 
former headquarters, Zhentil Keep, gather their 
mercenaries and build an army to overthrow all of 
Faerun.  
Can the heroes unravel the pla ns of the Zhentarim, 
unite the f actions and overthrow Pereghost in this 
epic campaign? 
 
 
 
HOW TO RUN THIS CAMPAIGN? 
 
The Rise of the Zhentarim is a sandbox campaign. 
You will use your own or third -party adventures to 
fill this campaign with life. Furthermore, you need to 
visualize most of the encounters of the main plot 
using maps and creatures, as you seem fit. 
 
This work provides a prologue to introduce the 
heroes to the campaign’s theme and an arc to follow 
on their heroic journey. You will also find tailored 
background information on the city of Neverwinter 
and the five involved factions. In the same chapter, 
you find an engaging method that describes how to 
integrate your own adventures into the thread of the 
campaign. Furthermore, I offer you the option of 
starting this campaign linearly and setting the heroes 
on their path using my three introductory 
adventures, which are purchasable at the DMs Guild. 
 
Obviously, you can omit or change any of the ideas 
presented here to suit your playing style and the way 
your campaign is taking. It’s a sandbox campaign. 
Make it your own! 
 
And one more thing …  I advise against telling the 
players the actual name of the campaign since it gives 
away some early plot developments. A more neutral 
title could be “Tales of the Sword Coast”. 
 
 
 
PROLOGUE 
 
Supply the players with the prologue  found on the 
following pages before the campaign starts and even 
before creating their characters. The  report of the 
last faction meeting will inform them about the 
conflict between the factions and set the tone for the 
campaign.

Hole in the Wall Gang - What happened so far? 
Shaena’s upcoming thieves’ guild, the Hole in Wall 
Gang, is part of the growing black Zhentarim 
network. Recently they kidnapped the half -elf 
diplomat and member of the Lords’ Alliance 
Immeral Brightwood, who just arrived from 
Silverymoon, and fed him to the aboleth in the 
Neverwinter chasm. Immeral wanted to report and 
discuss the increased crimes in Silverymoon and 
their connection to the black network - still 
unknown in Neverwinter - as well as debate possible 
actions of the Lord’s Alliance. 
 
 
The Hole in Wall Gang - First Contact 
The heroes want to investigate the disappearance of 
Immeral for their own reasons, which they should 
work out in the characters’ backstories. Immeral  
wanted to meet with all heroes partly because of their 
personal relationship and partly because he wanted 
to discuss his troubling messages with members of 
all four virtuous factions. Even though the heroes 
probably do not  know each other yet, he invited 
them all for the same time to the Driftwood Tavern 
before he traveled to Neverwinter.  
 
Read out  the following introduction to the first 
session to set the heroes on their path.

WHERE TO GO FROM HERE? 
 
Finishing the introductory adventures or creating 
your own start, the heroes should now be on their 
path. They are involved with one of the virtuous 
factions and have a personal agenda to gain influence 
and reconcile the faction s, in order to  expose and 
confront the Zhentarim with their combined help. 
The heroes should be motivated by themselves or 
nudged a little to visit their factions, which will offer 
them the missions to advance in renown and rank. 
This is where the sandbox aspect of the campaign 
comes into play. Use your adventure collection 6 or 
improvise your own to create an open world for the 
players, which gives them the feeling of freedom and 
choice. You can always lead them back to the main 
arc of the campaign, as you seem fit. 
 
It’s a sandbox campaign. Make it your own!
