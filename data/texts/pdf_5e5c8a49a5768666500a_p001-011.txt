INTRODUCTION
Once famous champion Ordark, who slew the
Lich King and single-handedly defeated the
dragon conclave (he couldn’t have done it any
other way, as he only has one arm), is now retired.
That doesn’t stop him from new endeavours such
as establishing “Adventures Daycare of Uncle
Ordark”, where he takes care of his fellow
adventurers’ children. Some just need him to
watch over their children while they travel across
the planes and assassinate gods. Others
unfortunately demised and left their offspring
alone. No matter how they got to his daycare,
Uncle Ordark will take care of them. His idea of a
daycare might be closer to the training camp of
the elite Thay legion, but where else would these
kids find a loving home and secure place to stay?
Whether they will use their acquired skill to
conquer the world or save it, Uncle Ordark will be
proud of them.
The players take the roles of Ordark’s proteges
around age 4-5 and will embark on a quest to
investigate Ordark’s disappearance.
STORY SYNAPSE
After Ordark puts the kids to bed, he is surprised
by the sudden visit of his ex-wife Talaryl Dalach.
They argue but realise that there is still something
between them and reforge the heat of their love
right there on the floor. That might have been
a little louder than they wanted, and Ordark is
scared they would wake the kids. So he takes a
few bottles of his best liquor and a food basket,
and then they head into the night.
Ordark Blood Fist is a giant of a half-orc in his
late 60s. His light green skin with greyish shades
is adorned by numerous tattoos and scars. His left
arm is missing from the elbow, but it doesn’t seem
to slow him down in any activity. In retirement,
Ordark is 11th-level Barbarian and 9th-level Cleric
with access to Raise Dead.
Taraly Dalach is a world-class assassin. She is
a drow and was raised to be a personal bodyguard
to the church of Spider Queen. After she met
Ordark, she left Underdark, travelled the world,
and built a name for herself as one of the best
assassins. One day she met Ordark again as a
guard to one of her marks. After she made him
unemployed, they got married and fought side by
side for several years.
The Kids are a mixed bag of orphans and kids
left in Ordark’s care as their parents headed into
danger. They are not ordinary children but
children of well-known adventurers and heroes,
so they often attract the attention of assassins
sent by revengeful foes of their parents or
kidnapper seeking hefty ransoms. Nothing that
Ordark cannot easily defeat without kids even
noticing.
ADVENTURE HOOK
Uncle Ordark tells his children a bedtime story about his
exploits in the Underdark: “...and I jumped into the pond
with the Elder Brain, and I started to pummel it with my fist.
When it realised that it could not get in my noggin as I was
filled with ANGER and RAGE, it panicked and tried to
strangle me with its tiny, puny tendrils. But my neck is too
thick for that! Ha, ha, ha. So I split it into one artistic elder
brain and one very logical, but both very much dead. Ha ha.
I couldn’t get rid of the brainy smell for months! Now it’s
time to sleep, my little warriors and remember Uncle
Ordark loves you above all else” he kisses their foreheads,
tucks them into the bed, dims the lantern, locks the door
and slowly descends the stairs stepping gingerly to make the
least amount of sound.
In the middle of the night, more perceptive kids
are woken by a sudden ruckus downstairs.
Muffled voices can be heard arguing about
something. Kids can try to see what is happening
through a hole in the floor. A high perception
check reveals that there are two figures. One is
Ordark, and the other figure is smaller in a dark
cloak. They shout at each other, and it seems
they start to fight and struggle over something.
Ordark seems to lose balance and falls to the
ground, grappled by a smaller figure. They
continue to “fight” and roll out of sight. Kids can
hear furniture breaking and screams and groan
like someone is being tortured down there. After a
few minutes, it stops, and all they can hear is the
sound of broken glass and the front door opening
and closing.
PLAY AS A KID
Each player should create a regular 3rd-level
character of any race and class. All characters are
children aged 1d3+2, no taller than 40 inches;
their movement is halved. At the start of the
adventure, they should have no equipment, only
simple clothes. The fact that characters are still
small children should be considered when
deciding on the Difficulty Classes (DC) of a
challenge. Even simple tasks can prove to be
difficult. For example: moving an armchair to the
window is a strength check with DC 15.

Kids start the adventure in the attic and need
to get to the basement, crawl through the window
into the garden and follow Ordark’s steps.
While kids stayed in Odarks care, they could
move around the house freely. Still, these parts
were off limits: basement, guest room, Ordarsk
study, Ordarsk bedroom, kitchen and backyard.
All doors in the house are heavy wooden doors
with iron reinforcement. The house’s walls are
stone, and the second floor and roof are made
entirely from wood.
The house itself has 2 floors and a basement.
Around the house is a lush garden with a white
gazebo and a small pond in the middle.
ATTIC
KIDS’ ROOM
A simply adorned room with three bunk beds, one
old wardrobe, chest, a small table, a couple of
stools and plenty of toys scattered around the floor.
The walls are decorated by poorly painted forest
critters, small birds, flowers and an occasional
beholder with a moustache and a hat or an aboleth
riding a bicycle. It seems like Ordark painted it
himself. There is one barred window with a view
of the backyard. The door is locked.

• On the table is a picture book about nature and
animals, illustrating different concepts: above

GUEST ROOM
The guest room is very dark as the only window is
covered with a bed sheet. The window is broken,
and the sheet occasionally moves with the outside
wind letting a few rays of light inside. The
characters without darkvision can only see the
outline of the window. A musty smell permeates
the surroundings, and everything is covered in a
thick layer of dust. The room is filled with random
pieces of furniture and equipment (chairs,
lantern, a coil of heavy rope, crates, cartwheel,
etc), rendering the room a difficult terrain (player
movement is halved).
In the corner is a small armour stand with a full
plate amour . In the middle of the chest plate is
the Cornucopia (horn of plenty), a symbol of the
halfling deity Yondalla.
As the players approach the middle of the room
or try to remove the sheet from the window, they
are ambushed by two Spiders (use stat blocks for
Giant Spider, Basic Rules pg. 136), which lower
themselves from the ceiling.
The door is unlocked and leads to the hallway.
From the hall, there is access to the Kids’ room
(locked with a latch), the Ordurk’s bedroom
(locked, key required), the Guest room and a
staircase.
HALLWAY
The hallway has a few paintings of different
adventuring parties led by Ordurk through the
years. There are short descriptions under the
paintings:
• “Daring Company and defeat of the Lich King
Szass Tam” - Ordurk stands in the middle
holding a grinning skull. Next to him stands a
dark elf woman with a hand over his shoulder.
• “..... “ Ordurk with an enormous battle axe on
his shoulder, some bearded wizard with a pipe
and big red hat, dwarven women and a bald
human with face tattoo stand in front of a corpse
of a beholder.
STAIRCASE
The staircase is trapped. The trap can be found
with an investigation/perception check with DC
15 and disarmed with DC 10. If a trap is triggered,
an enormous battle axe (depicted in the painting)
swings from the ceiling at the height of an adult
person (good 20 inches above the players’ heads).
At the bottom of the stairs is a Living room.
FIRST FLOOR
LIVING ROOM
The living room is the largest room on the first
floor. A front door is currently locked and cannot
be opened. All windows on the first floor are barred
with heavy iron bars. From the living room, a
kitchen can be entered. Next to the kitchen is a
door to the Ordark’s study.
In the middle of the living room is a broken
coffee table; next to it is a toppled armchair, torn
Ordark’s shirt with a little bit of blood and
Ordark’s belt can be found under the sofa. Kids
never saw Ordark without his favourite belt, even
though it had no effect on him. Next to the sofa, a
dagger with a jagged obsidian blade with a silver
spider on the handle (history check: it is related
to drow) is sticking from the floor.

ORDARK ’S STUDY
It is a poorly lit room with nice, heavy furniture.
In the middle is a desk with piles of paper. Most of
them are letters starting with a name and address,
but the rest is scribbled out. On the floor is a pile
of crumpled letters that smell like Ordarks cologne.
Behind the desk is a comfortable leather armchair
and bookcase filled with various tomes. Otherwise,
the room is filled with memorabilia: Golden Crown,
Dragon tooth, . . .
On the left side are two aquariums. One of them
is broken. In the other is a grey cube with a
golden key in the middle . If disturbed or the
players try to reach for the key, the cube attacks
(see Gray Ooze, Basic Rules, pg. 338.).
On the right side of the room are two identical
chests with golden locks. One of the chests is a
mimic (see Basic Rules, pg. 332) that escaped
from the aquarium.
Next to the chests is a liquor cabinet with doors
still open, and one bottle is broken in front of
it. One bottle in the cabinet is different from the
others. It is a bottle of Holy Water.
The golden key unlocks the actual chest, and
inside is: Bag of tricks (see Basic Rules, 154),
Circlet of Blasting (see Basic Rules, pg, 158),
Potion of healing .
On the table is a small copper box (which can
be unlocked with a copper key from the fireplace).
There is a picture of Ordark with a Drow woman
in a white dress inside the copper box. Next to the
image is a ring made out of dark reddish metal.

BASEMENT
The basement is cold, and the air is humid and
stale. The basement is filled with barrels and bags
with rations. On the other side is a barred door
similar to a prison cell. Inside the cell are various
crates and bags. Above them is a small, fully open
window. The size is suitable for the players. The
cell can be opened by pulling a chain. The chain
is heavy, and all players must join in the effort.
As the barred door moves slightly, arcane marks
appear on the floor. They start to shine, crack and
vanish with a sizzle. The door flies up on its own
with a loud crash.
“Finally free! It’s been a long time since we
talked, my dear FRIEND!” Resonates in the mind
of all players. The voice is pleasant and sweet, and
the overall demeanour is kind until it breaks into
a screech.
With the flash of the light, one of the crates
breaks open. Splinters of wood fly in all directions.
Torches on the wall are ablaze, and the room is
illuminated in the brightest light.
A skull draped in pale, dry tattooed skin flies
in the air. Its white eyes covered in yellowish
film start frantically searching the room. The jaw
unhinges, and maniacal cackle resonates through
the air and minds.
“What are YOU? Children! I have to thank thee
from the depths of my existence! My little saviours!
Of course, I shall be eternally magnanimous and
grant you the greatest gift of all. I will undo your
PITIFUL existence! ” For stat block, see Appendix.
After defeating the head of the lich, Players can
climb out the window and access the garden.
