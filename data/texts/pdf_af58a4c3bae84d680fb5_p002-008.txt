The Silver Bane Campaign
The Amphail Affair
Introduction: Seeking a rest from travel, the Party arrives in Amphail, looking forward to a 
warm meal and a refreshing drink. The villagers gather as the night falls and a friendly, 
peaceful night begins. The calm of the evening is soon shattered as a woman rushes into the 
tavern, screaming hysterically.
A 2-hour adventure intended for 1st-3rd level characters
by Callum Williamson

This encounter is intended to be the introductory 
session of a homebrew campaign beginning in 
Amphail and contains references to homebrew rules 
for that campaign, which are included in the 
'Homebrew Rules' section. 
It can be run as a one-shot for any running 
campaign by placing it in any small village. A lot of 
information and choices are given here as there is an 
assumption that a first time or beginner DM will be 
running the encounter, who may not be as 
comfortable being 'on-the-fly', there are multiple 
branching points and opportunities for the DM to 
bring the Party back to the intended plot but also 
examples of consequences that you can implement in 
‘The Worst Possible Outcome’ section. 
There are multiple options for the DM to sidestep 
difficult combat with NPC actions to allow this to run 
with as little as 1 DM and 1 Player.
Running as part of The 
Silver Bane Campaign
Key things to remember if running as part of the 
'Silver Bane':
 The Party should learn about Lycanthropy, 
Belladonna, and V ariscite.
 The Party will almost certainly not be able to 
kill the lycanthropes when they are at full 
health and not under the effect of Belladonna / 
V ariscite. A single attack from a wererat could 
kill a character with too low of a total health 
pool in a single hit, especially at level 1. 
 The woodsman does not use the wererat stat 
block when he is in human form, this is 
because he has never transformed before, but 
he will transform if his health reaches 0 HP . 
 Saving villagers is directly tied to their reward. 
 Remember about Passive skills, if a check is 
equal to or lower than a Characters base stat 
and modifier, maybe allow them to 
automatically succeed. 
 If anyone drops to 0 HP , and no one was 
prepared to have their character die in the first 
session, ensure their body is evacuated by 
either a fleeing villager or another Party 
member, this could bring them back into the 
fold with 1 HP or they just survive for the next 
session if it happens towards the end. You can 
even hide and fiddle with the death throws. 
 ‘The Worst Possible Outcome’ section is for a 
Party that is just not engaging with the session, 
blundering so hard that you need to kill some 
villagers, or are able to damage the wererats 
significantly and you want to include a fight.
The Party Arrive
Text within boxes like below is meant to be read out 
loud, dice rolls and other important information will 
be bold.
Each member of the party has come to Amphail to rest 
after long journeys, congregating one by one in the 
Amphail Ale, they sit at a shared table.
The atmosphere in the tavern is welcoming, the fire 
warm, the food and drink refreshing after your 
journey. It is a peaceful evening, the local shopkeepers 
and villagers begin to congregate in the tavern as the 
day comes to a close, soon the whole town is gathered, 
18 people crowd the tavern as a lively night begins.
Take this opportunity to have the Party introduce 
their characters, perhaps they speak to the villagers.
Investigate disturbances on the road leading between 
Amphail and Waterdeep. Someone mentions that they 
have been waiting for a letter for some time, and 
another says it has been a few days since any 
merchants travelled through town.
The foresters have been finishing work early, so they 
are home long before nightfall.

Intelligence (Investigation) DC 12 / Wisdom 
(Perception DC 12) / Wisdom (Insight) DC 12 – The 
Character notices a woman fussing over a woodsman's 
bandage in the corner, he is trying to rest. When asked 
about it he will say it was just from an accident with 
an axe but the woman won't believe him.
If pressed to see the wound, Charisma (Persuasion / 
Intimidation) DC 12 the party can try to examine it.
 
Wisdom (Nature) DC 12, they can observe the wound 
is actually a bite mark, they are unable to tell which 
animal caused it, but they can tell it has a large 
mouth from the puncture pattern. 
Intelligence (Medicine) DC 12, they can tell the wound 
is infected and that it likely from an animal attack.
In truth it doesn’t matter what the Party decides to do 
at this point, before they can do anything further a 
villager bursts into the tavern screaming.
The welcoming and relaxing atmosphere slowly dies 
as muffled screams come from outside.
Suddenly the door to the Amphail Ale slams open as a 
woman bursts into the tavern screaming in terror.
"It's here! oh gods! the monster is here! It killed Delvin!" 
The woman cries, collapsing to the ground as she 
enters the safety of the tavern.
"Valiria? What in the hells are you talking about?" 
another villager asks, as they rush over to her, urging 
her to sit down at a table.
The Party watch as the other villagers immediately 
split into two groups, some heading outside to 
investigate and the others crowding round the 
woman, a majority stay.
At this point the Party will decide what they will do, 
will they stay and hear what Valiria has to say or will 
they go outside to investigate?
If the party interacted with the woodsman, they look 
around at the scene before them and notice he is even 
more on edge, he hasn’t moved from his chair and he 
is staring at Valiria, seemingly terrified.

Staying in the tavern
The Party has decided to stay in the tavern, or have 
returned after being outside and you decided to allow 
them to return with no consequences, and speak with 
the occupants.
The Party approaches the table where Valiria is sitting 
and hear the following:
"Slow down Valiria, breathe, tell us what happened" 
the bartender says, trying to soothe her.
"He... He's dead... I... There was so much blood" Valiria 
sobs into her hands.
The Party realises she is nearly inconsolable in her 
current state.
The Party may try to speak to Valiria, the woodsman, 
or head outside at this point. 7 villagers leave the 
tavern to investigate and 11 villagers stay, including 
the barman, the woodsman, and Valiria.
Speaking to Valiria
Valiria is sitting at a table surrounded by the 
remaining villagers.
Wisdom (Insight) DC 10 – The Party successfully sees 
that Valiria is terrified to her core, there won't be an 
easy way to calm her down without a good deal of 
convincing or a shock to her senses. (Meant to gently 
nudge the players towards a solution)
Charisma (Persuasion) DC 12 or Charisma 
(Intimidation) DC 15 – The Party successfully 
manages to calm her down. After some shuddering 
breathes Valiria explains what happened, "My 
husband Delvin... I went outside to fetch him for 
supper and... all I found were his clothes, ripped to 
shreds on the ground, great chunks of his skin... 
everywhere..."
If pressed for details, all she will add is "I told 
them I wasn't lying... I told them I saw it... now he's 
dead...", she begins sobbing again at this point and 
will not elaborate unless Calm Person is cast on her or 
the Party get her a drink (does not have to be a strong 
drink).
If Calm Person is cast or she is given a drink, 
she settles down and says "It was the monster in the 
forest, I don't know what it was but I saw it when I 
was foraging for belladonna last month. I tried to tell 
the others but they just ignored me and said I was 
spooked by a boar."
Characters proficient in Nature are told that 
belladonna is a poisonous plant that can hurt them if 
handled incorrectly, by making an Intelligence 
(Nature) DC 10 ability check. It's used to make an a 
numbing salve. They note that her fingers and hands 
have hives on them, a reaction to touching the plant.
If the Party asks why the creature didn't attack 
her, she doesn't know.
Wisdom (Medicine) DC 10 – The Party inspects 
Valiria for injuries. There are no injuries on Valeria, 
the blood she is covered in is presumably from Delvin. 
The blood is on her face, hands, and chest.
Physical Strike – The Party decides to physically 
shock Valiria.
Slap – Strength DC 14, a Character slaps 
Valiria to shock her into calmness.
On a normal failure, she recoils and cries 
harder, she is inconsolable now, the barkeep strongly 
suggests that the Party leave her alone, he will not be 
persuaded to let them speak to her, nor would she 
speak to the Party if they were somehow able to get to 
her.
On a critical fail, or just if you think it will be 
entertaining, the slap knocks her out, the villagers 
around rush to her and chastise the Party, ordering 
them to leave the tavern, leading them to the scene 
were Greela is just about to see the monster. This can 
have a small effect for the ‘The Transformation’ section

Speaking to the Woodsman
If the party spoke to woodsman before, they are now 
able to insist he explains his injury as they notice how 
nervous he now is that Valiria is here, and he is sitting 
alone in the corner.
Charisma (Persuasion / Intimidation) DC 15 – If the 
party already discovered that the wound is a bite 
mark they have Advantage on this roll. The Party 
convinces the woodsman to detail what happened to 
him.
The woodsman whispers, "Fine I'll tell you, but 
keep your voices down... Last night I was coming 
home, I hadn't paid any mind to the stories people 
round here were spreading, but then I saw it... 
something in the corner of my eye, watching me... I 
could see it creeping between the trees, something as 
big as I am, hairy too, and a disgusting, fleshy tail... 
Anyway I start sprinting and chomp!... I dig my 
fingers into it's eyes before I could even think, the beast 
let go of me and I ran as fast as I could... The thing 
didn't chase after me... I thought that would be the end 
of it... I should have told someone…"
 If the Party has not already inspected the 
wound, the woodsman lets them examine it. They do 
not need to roll anything here as he tells them that the 
wound is infected.

Physically Threaten – The Party physically threatens 
the woodsman to reveal what happened to him. As he 
is injured and scared, the Party will automatically 
succeed at this.
The woodsman begs the Party not to hurt him. 
This draws some villagers away from Valiria. "Okay, 
Okay, I'll tell you" The rest is the same as persuading 
him except he now also apologises to the villagers and 
Valiria directly, "I'm sorry I didn't believe you Valiria, 
I'm so sorry...”
Valiria just nods solemnly in response.
The Party might be convinced to keep an eye on the 
woodsman or restrain him, if the want to do this 
allow it, but have them roll the following. 
Charisma (Persuasion) DC 16, with Advantage if 
they uncovered his story AND the possible connection 
to Lycanthropy – You successfully convince the 
villagers that it would be a good idea to restrain the 
woodsman as you think he is infected.
If the result is 18 or higher, the woodsman 
allows you to do this. Otherwise, roll a Strength 
(Athletics) DC 16 to forcefully do so.
The villagers will react with disdain toward the 
Party if they forcefully retrain the woodsman as they 
failed to convince them it was necessary, possibly 
making them leave the tavern.
Speaking to the villagers
Should the Party decide to speak to other patrons of 
the bar, before speaking to Valiria, then no one has 
any idea what is going on as they are also trying to 
find out what is happening, they will shoo the Party as 
they try to eavesdrop or speak to Valiria themselves, 
some example statements are shown below.
"Shh! I can't hear what she's saying."
"Did you see how much blood is on her?"
"Hells, I can't fathom what would do this."
"Poor Valiria, she looks scared to death."
If the Party only managed to get the basic information 
from Valiria, the other patrons will be shocked and 
sympathetic towards Valeria.
"Oh gods, Delvin is dead!"
"What could have done this?"
"To shreds she said."
If the Party managed to get Valiria to talk about the 
monster, the other patrons will shuffle around 
nervously with guilty expressions.
"She came running into the village shouting about 
some hairy beast she saw, we thought she was just 
spooked, had we have known…"
"I'm so sorry Valiria, we... we should have believed 
you…"
"We didn't listen and now... oh poor Delvin…"
"I mean people told stories... but to think they are real... 
it's terrifying."
None of the villagers are aware of the woodsman's 
injury and will offer next to no details.
"Oh that sort of thing happens all the time... between 
the wolves, boars, and whatever else lives in there, 
someone always comes home with a scratch or two"
"I don't know why he'd lie about it being an axe 
wound... excuse me I need to see if Valiria is okay"
If the Party got Valiria to reveal about the monster, 
"Surely it wasn't from the same beast? Why would he 
hide it?"
They do not leave
If the Party spends too much time in the tavern, by 
either exhausting their talking points with Valiria and 
the woodsman, and speaking with too many villagers, 
or by lingering to see what happens (roughly 5 to 10 
minutes of in game time), or just doesn't go outside, 
then the plot advances.
The door slams open again and the villagers who left 
earlier return, though dishevelled, exhausted and 
terrified.
 "It was there! It was outside!" a villager shouts, "Just at 
the edge of the village, standing and staring at us!"
 "Greela went over to scare it away and it mauled his 
arms and bit his neck! The poor whoreson was dead in 
an instant" another villager adds.
 "Elimar threw a pitchfork at it and flew into the 
beasts chest, but it pulled it out and threw it to the 
ground... the thing scurried away but it didn't seem 
injured." a third villager continues.

"It was the size of a man and as hairy as a wolf, 
with a twisted, fleshy tail" Elimar confirms.
The villagers are panicking now, being non-
combatants, everyone is Frightened of the monster. 
They are refusing to go outside again and are at a loss 
about what to do, some suggest locking the doors and 
windows and hiding, some others suggest fleeing the 
village entirely.
The Party can attempt to rally the villagers 
with a Charisma (Persuasion) DC14 ability roll, 
which removes the 'Can't Approach' effect of 
Frightened. If they roll a DC18 or higher, the 
Frightened effect is totally removed and the DM could 
award the Character that gives the speech the 
'Amphail Alliance' Heroic Inspiration point.
The Party might suggest leaving the tavern to 
investigate, if this happens proceed to the ‘Leave the 
Tavern’ section, and allow them to make the checks 
found at the start of that section other than the 
Perception check to see the creature as it has already 
gone.
"Be careful out there, come back and tell us if 
you find anything, the monster ran into the woods but 
keep watch and be safe." one of the villagers that was 
outside says to the Party.
If the Party returns from their investigation, stays in 
the tavern, or decides to flee, move onto 'The 
Woodsman Turns' section. This could be a branching 
point for 'The Worst Possible Outcome' section.
