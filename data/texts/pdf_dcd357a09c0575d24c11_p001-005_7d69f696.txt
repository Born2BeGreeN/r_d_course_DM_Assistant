The Curse of Skjaarl
MYSTERIOUS  CURSE  HAS  TRAPPED  THE  VILLAGERS
of the tiny village of Skjaarl, with no hope of

Adventure Summary
Those who live in the village of Skjaarl, or dare venture
there, become trapped by an evil curse that has taken
hold there no more than a few weeks prior. Once within
half a mile of the village it is impossible to leave the
area, immediately ending back up on one of the roads
leading into the village, no matter where or how far they
may attempt to travel. It is up to the adventurers to
break the curse and allow everyone to freely leave the
village and its surrounding area once more.
Once inside the village the adventurers will face a
small band of Mephits (small imp-like creatures)) that
blame the villagers and have been regularly attacking
the townsfolk in an attempt to end the curse that has
also been plaguing them. After dealing with the attack
the mayor will explain the curse and the adventurers too
are now trapped. They must investigate the townsfolk
and surroundings of the village to find the source of the
curse, as well as the culprit(s) responsible.
After a thorough investigation the adventurers will be
able to determine that the curse has been cast by
Melinda Bellemund, one of the villagers. She is a
woman scorned and cast the curse to punish her
husband and the woman with whom he was cheating.
Upon either confronting or attacking her she will fight
back to try and kill either her husband, the other
woman, or the adventurers themselves if they get in the
way.
After she has been defeated, either by killing, exiling,
or otherwise incapacitating her the adventurers will be
rewarded for their help, and will also be able to continue
their travels, no longer constrained by the bindings of
the curse.
Introduction
The village of Skjaarl is located somewhere deep in a
dark and large forest, in a relatively hard to reach place,
only visited by travelers who know it’s there, or by those
who become hopelessly lost. Its somewhat obscured
location comes from a desire of the townspeople to be
free of the turmoil of the big cities, and to live a calm life
in which they provide for themselves, free of the
constraints of urban living. The village has existed for a
long time, and has never had more than twenty-five
inhabitants at once, in current day dwindling to a mere
ten.
The village has a small inn mostly used as a gathering
place for the locals after a day of hard work, but also
functioning as a place of refuge for travelers passing by.
There is no general store in the village, as the
community is too small to sustain such a business. The
various townspeople do however have small shops
outside their homes where they sell some basic
foodstuffs, artworks, and tools to both their neighbors
and visitors. These shops are nothing more than a little
stall with a small amount of merchandise and a wooden
box in which people can drop money to pay for their
purchases.
While the village is usually an oasis of peace, recently
it has suffered some serious problems, troubling both
locals and visitors alike. The last few weeks any who
have attempted to leave the village have found that they
will start feeling dizzy and slowly blackout after half a
mile or so, after which they suddenly find themselves
back on one of the two roads leading into the village as
if they were just arriving. Since the locals have little to
no knowledge of magic, they are unsure how to deal
with this seemingly magical problem, and they have
been trapped in their village for weeks. Travelers and
visitors simply passing through have found themselves
in the same predicament, and are forced to reside in the
local inn, unable to leave the village. As if these
problems were not bad enough, a roaming group of
Mephits got stuck in the curse as well, and is blaming
the townsfolk. They’ve periodically been attacking the
townspeople since they believe them to be responsible,
but so far this has not had much effect. They have been
present near the village since the start of the curse,
fueling speculation amongst the locals that the Mephits
have something to do with the current troubles.
Running the adventure
The Curse of Skjaarl is an adventure for three to four
adventurers of 2nd level. At the end of the adventure
they would level up to 3rd level using the milestone
experience system, should you want to use the same
characters for another future module. The various
combat statistics of the monsters and people used in
this adventure can all be found at the bottom of the
module. The same goes for any magical items that may
be found.
Adjusting difficulty
Each encounter is tailored toward a group of three to
four adventurers of 2nd level with only limited
experience and knowledge about D&D. Each encounter
also offers two different difficulties (both easier and
harder) to adjust the combat intensity to your liking.

Overview
While the village is pretty much the only setting in
which this module will take place, the story can roughly
be divided up into three parts:
In part 1 the adventurers will enter the village and
deal with an attack of Mephits.
In part 2 the adventurers will learn about the curse
from the local people and start their investigation.
In part 3 the adventurers will locate the source of the
curse and lift it by defeating the person(s) or
creature(s) responsible.
Adventure hooks
The hook for your adventurers to solve this quest is
relatively simple. The village of Skjaarl is in the middle
of a big forest, and can easily be used as a short resting
point for travelers going from one big city to another, or
for those who have simply become lost. Once inside the
village the adventurers will be stuck there themselves
until they lift the curse, forcing them to participate in the
module.
Should you so wish you can always come up with a
more distinct reason why the adventurers are traveling
from one place to another to match with an ongoing
campaign, or fit a specific setting.

Part 1: Welcome to Skjaarl
HE  ADVENTURE  BEGINS  WITH  THE  ADVENTURERS
traveling through the forest en route to a
destination of their choice. During their
journey through the forest you can have
them interact with each other to introduce
their characters, and explain their reasons
for traveling. Once the introductions have
been made and everyone is ready to begin the module,
read the following text:
Your calm and peaceful journey through the woods is
suddenly interrupted by the barely noticeable but
distinct sounds of combat in the distance. Somewhat
worried you exchange a glance with your fellow
adventurers, before drawing your weapons and heading
towards the noise. You pass a slight bend in the road
and suddenly find yourselves on the outskirts of a very
small village in a clearing in the woods. There can be no
more than eight buildings here, most of them positioned
in half a circle around a larger building which you
immediately deduce must be the local inn. At the centre
of the village is an old-fashioned water well behind
which an older man, bloodied and bruised, is seeking
refuge from multiple flying imp-like creatures. As you
near the village the man sees you and waves his sword
at you, calling out for help. He pays dearly for trying to
draw your attention as one of the flying creatures
manages to land a clean hit on the man’s head,
knocking him out cold. Now with the old man down the
creatures turn around and charge at you!
Combat Encounter
The adventurers will face off against a number of
Mephits (determined by the difficulty you choose). Once
just one of the Mephits is brought to 0 hit points it will
retain its consciousness but will flee away along with
the other Mephits, leaving the adventurers alone with
the unconscious older man.
Mephits come in various forms (Dust, Ice, Steam, and
Magma). Feel free to determine at random what kind of
Mephits are used (even multiple different ones). Take
note that Steam Mephits are slightly weaker than the
other types.
Easy encounter: 2 Mephits.
Average encounter: 3 Mephits. (Recommended.)
Hard encounter: 4 Mephits.
The Village of Skjaarl
Once the fight has concluded, read the following
passage:
As the last of the Mephits flies off with the motionless
body of one of its kin in his hands, you suddenly hear a
door fly open, and rapid footsteps. As you turn you see a
large woman with long braids and a rather round face
waving you inside. She yells at you to bring the
unconscious man and after you’ve managed to pick him
up and drag him to the inn, she quickly closes the door
behind you.
The innkeeper is called Freya Wildeheart and she will
welcome the adventurers in town, thank them for saving
the village and the old man, and will offer them board
and sustenance as a reward for their assistance. She
will go on to explain the predicament the village faces,
and explains the curse, pointing out that by entering the
village the adventurers themselves have now also
become trapped in the curse that has been plaguing the
villagers. She shares this information with the
adventurers while tending to the wounds of the old man,
whom she identifies as Hagar Steelhammer, a retired
adventurer who has been their main line of defense
against the attacks of the Mephits. The imp-like pests
have been attacking the villagers believing them to be
responsible for the curse, and trapping them in the
woods with no way of escape.
From this point on the adventurers are stuck in the
village, and trying to leave the village will cause them to
blackout after about half a mile, after which they will
find themselves back on one of the two roads leading
into the village.
