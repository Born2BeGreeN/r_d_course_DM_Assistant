Winds of Betrayal: Shadows in the Citadel of Ice and Steel 
 
Introduction: Prepare to embark on a journey into the elemental city, the Citadel of Ice and Steel. 
Your players will unravel deceit, treachery, and an elemental cult as they navigate the floating 
metropolis. With an enigmatic heist in the background, hidden secrets near a Djinn noble, and 
the menacing presence of the Howling Hatred cult. As they untangle the threads of the narrative, 
they'll delve into a world rich with elemental lore, enigmatic NPCs, and unique challenges, all set 
against the backdrop of a city suspended in perpetual freefall. Are your players ready to uncover 
the mysteries lurking in the skies and bring evil cultists to justice?

The Invitation 
 
 
 
Suddenly, a hushed whisper of arcane magic ripples near the 
group, followed by the subtle chime of a tuning fork. You turn 
to see a flicking white and blue magical gate materialize 
before you. 
 
Emerging gracefully from the shimmering portal is an 
aarakocra, resembling a vibrant cardinal in both appearance 
and stature. He extends a polite wing in greeting, his beady 
eyes glittering with intelligence. 
 
At first the aarakocra speaks to the players in Auran. 
If no one responds, he switches to Common. 
 
"Greetings, travelers," he chirps in a melodic voice. "I am 
Zephyrus, a humble servant of a Djinn noble Azurith of the 
enigmatic Citadel of Ice and Steel. My master extends a 
gracious invitation to you, esteemed guests, for a 
forthcoming celebratory banquet." 
 
Zephyrus delicately unfurls a small, intricately patterned 
envelope and offers it to your group. The envelope is 
adorned with intricate designs that seem to dance and shift, 
capturing your attention. He continues, "Please, accept this 
invitation, and you shall find yourselves transported to the 
main portal gate of the Citadel in one tenday. Do wear formal 
attire, for the occasion calls for it." 
 
He pauses for a moment, a glimmer of concern flashing in his 
avian eyes. "One thing, however," Zephyrus adds cautiously, 
"the magic within the envelope is a one-time use. I implore 
you not to open it prematurely, as it will only activate when 
our gracious host welcomes his esteemed guests." 
 
Zephyrus, dressed in the regal attire befitting a servant of a 
noble, awaits your response. 
 
A DC 15 Insight (Wisdom) check may reveal that the 
aarakocra is not entirely truthful about the nature of the 
envelope. Opening it early or after the banquet's conclusion 
will not activate the magic but will reveal an invitation to 
another group of adventurers. 
 
A casting of detect magic on the envelope reveals an 
aura of conjuration magic.

Getting Ready 
 
The characters have a tenday to prepare for the 
grand banquet, which includes various tasks to 
ensure they are ready for the festivities. One of the 
key preparations is acquiring formal attire, as they 
must adhere to the dress code for the occasion. 
 
As you accept the invitation from Zephyrus, you 
realize that you have precisely a tenday to prepare 
for the upcoming grand banquet at the Citadel of Ice 
and Steel. The tasks ahead are twofold, and the clock 
is ticking. 
 
1. Formal Attire: The invitation clearly states that 
formal attire is expected at the celebration. If you 
already possess suitable garments, you're one step 
ahead. However, if you lack the appropriate clothing, 
you'll need to find or acquire it within the tenday. 
The palace will be opulent, so your attire should 
match the grandeur of the event. 
 
2. Banquet Preparations: Aside from attire, you 
should also consider any other preparations you 
might need for travelling on another plane. Perhaps 
there are items or spells you wish to acquire, or 
information you want to gather about the Citadel, its 
inhabitants, or the Djinn noble himself. It would be a 
good idea to be able to speak or at least understand 
Auran. 
 
What is Going On 
 
Eleven months ago, Howling Hatred cultist stole the 
Scepter of Zephyrion from Azurith's palace. They did 
this by bribing Zephyrus to steal the scepter and 
deliver it to them in the Citadel of Ice and Steel. 
Azurith hired a group of adventurers to recover the 
scepter. The heroes followed the cultist to a 
windswept mountain near the Dessarin Valley and 
defeated the Howling Hatred Priest in charge of a 
clandestine safe house. The heroes triumphantly 
returned the scepter to Azurith and took a short 
vacation in the Citadel of Ice and Steel. The cultists 
blackmailed Zephyrus. Under the cultists' coercion, 
Zephyrus lured the heroes into a trap, where the 
cultists murdered the adventurers. 
 
Not knowing the fate of the heroes, Azurith instructs 
Zephyrus to invite them to his annual celebratory 
banquet. Desperate not to get caught, Zephyrus 
invited the first group of adventurers on the Material 
Plane he could find. Zephyrus, however, knows well 
that Azurith's eyes cannot discern the true heroes 
from the newcomers, for in his eyes, "All 
mudwalkers look the same." Zephyrus will try to 
keep the players away from Azurith, and usher them 
out of the palace as early as possible. If the party 
misbehaves or if he gets desperate, Zephyrus will 
accuse the players of some crime and have security 
escort the players to a coach or subdue the players. 
 
Guests assume that the players are the heroes.

Traveling to the 
palace 
 
 
 
As the appointed time arrives and the players open 
the patterned invitation. 
 
A sudden and enchanting sensation washes over you. The air 
fills with the harmonious hum of a tuning fork, and before 
your eyes, a flickering, mesmerizing blue and white light 
emerges, swirling and expanding to form a ten-foot diameter 
circular portal. 
 
Stepping through the portal, you find yourselves standing just 
outside the massive gate of the Citadel of Ice and Steel. The 
grand citadel looms before them, its towering walls glistening 
with icy elegance, while steel structures rise like sentinels 
against the azure sky. 
 
But their attention is quickly drawn as a voice calls 
out in Auran, from behind and to the right. 
Characters who cannot understand Auran do not 
understand the coach driver. 
 
"You must be the esteemed mudwalkers from the Material 
Planes," says a female air genasi. She motions for you to step 
onto a magnificent coach that hovers above the icy ground. 
The carriage has no wheels but floats gracefully, pulled by a 
pair of majestic, white-winged griffons. 
 
Outside the citadel's walls, a line of similar coaches awaits, all 
poised to transport guests to Azurith's majestic palace. 
 
As your journey to the palace begins, you are treated to 
breathtaking aerial views of the Citadel of Ice and Steel. 
However, the serenity is short-lived as a dark and foreboding 
storm cloud materializes with alarming speed. Lightning arcs 
through the skies, followed by thunderous claps that 
reverberate through the very marrow of your bones. 
 
The blinding flashes of lightning and deafening roars 
of thunder surround the carriage, and all players 
need to roll two saving throws. 
 
• DC 10 Constitution saving throw or be deafened by the 
storm.  
• DC 10 Constitution saving throw to avoid being 
temporarily blinded by the lightning. 
 
These effects persist for two minutes.

Azurith’s Palace 
 
 
 
The players' journey through the tempestuous skies 
eventually leads them to a sight that takes their 
breath away. Perched majestically upon a hovering 
island is the resplendent palace of Azurith, 
surrounded by a sprawling ranch devoted to the 
training and care of griffons. 
 
As you prepare to disembark from your magnificent coach, 
your eyes take in the sprawling ranch. It's a bustling hub of 
activity, where stables, pens, and soaring perches are 
scattered across the fertile terrain. Griffons of various sizes 
and colors roam, train, and take flight, showcasing a 
remarkable display of grace and power. The air is filled with 
the resonant calls and melodic cries of these majestic 
creatures. 
 
The significance of this ranch becomes apparent—the source 
of Azurith's wealth lies in the breeding, raising, and training 
of griffons, which serve both as steeds and essential beasts of 
burden in the skies. 
 
As the coach lands on the floating earth mote, an aarakocra 
clad in impeccable formal attire approaches. His attire exudes 
an air of regality, befitting a servant of Azurith.  
 
With a graceful bow of his avian head, he greets the 
party in Auran. 
 
"Welcome, honored guests," with a mixture of 
reverence and warmth. He motions for the players to 
follow him, inviting them to enter the palace and 
partake in the grandeur that awaits within.
