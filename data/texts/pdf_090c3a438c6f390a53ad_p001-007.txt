Welcome to “The Pumpkin King”, a free 
Halloween encounter from DM Games! 
Unlike our full modules, this is a one shot 
encounter that can be dropped into an 
existing campaign or the springboard to 
start one. 
This encounter takes place in the dreaded 
domain of Ravenloft, however it can be 
dropped into any one of D&D’s many 
settings or one of your own invention. This 
adventure is for 4 PCs level 1 - 2 
All you NEED for this adventure is this book 
and a copy of the Dungeons & Dragons 
basic rules which are able to be 
downloaded for free from playdnd.com. and 
a set of gaming dice. I would also Highly 
recommend the SRD available from the 
same sight as it has a COMPLETE listing of 
all D&D Rules. 
In addition, I would also recommend some 
miniatures and an erasable battle mat, but 
those are completely optional. Our maps 
What is needed to Play?
Introduction
The small farming village of Krahedorf, if 
you can call their meager corn crops 
“farms”, has had a rash of children go 
missing leading up to all hallows eve. Even 
for Barovia this is not a common problem. 
Speaking to the villagers its become 
apparent that their is something sinister in 
the area. 
The creature taking the children has come 
to be known as “The Great Pumpkin”. He 
takes many forms but favors that of a 
scarecrow as he can easily replace the 
many other regular rag doll manequins, 
Encounter Synopsis
The Punkin’, as he is sometimes know 
as, will send envoys of his “children” into 
town during the revels leading up to all 
hallows eve and spread rumors of sweets 
and gifts in the corn field to lure his 
unsuspecting victims to him. Once there, 
the children go missing and become his 
minions for eternity, consuming the souls 
required for him to return yet another 
year and feast.  
The Great Pumpkin has no motivation 
other than to feed and revel in the souls 
of the stolen children for yet another year. 
Such is the perversion of a demon
The Adventure Begins
Read the following boxed text to the 
players to start the Adventure:
When you entered Krahedorf Village, 
you were ready to take a rest from your 
travels. It had been a hard road, and 
stops between your journey’s 
departure point and destination have 
been few and far between, but such is 
the life of a new adventurer.  
While enjoying your meal, well at least 
having…”enjoying” is a strong word in 
this case, you and your party are 
approached by several haggard and 
sorrow weary villagers. They tell you a 
tale of how their children have got 
missing and they suspect “The Great 
Pumpkin” or “The Punkin’” as one 
particularly dim witted villager calls 
him, has lured the children to the 
cornfields with promises of treats and 
gifts.

Feeling particularly heroic, you 
promise the worried villagers that you 
will go to the cornfield and will not 
return without their children
The Cornfield
The Players arrive at the cornfield and on 
your typical cold “dark and stormy nights” 
so familiar in every halloween and horror 
stories. And while this is designed as a 
“tongue in cheek” halloween adventure, it 
should be treated some what seriously. It 
IS a horror story after all, and as the 
players will soon discover, the “Minions” 
they are fighting are what is left of the 
children after “The Punkin’” gets a hold of 
them.  
Read the text of the players:
As you approach the cornfield an 
ominous feeling settles over you. The 
wind picks up and you are almost 
instantly chilled to the bone. Between 
the icy gale and the mist that begins to 
saturate your clothes, you swear you 
can almost feel your body temperature 
drop.  
Looking around, you find a path that 
leads deeper into the cornfield and 
realize it is in fact a corn maze. When 
you venture forth, blue-white lightening 
splits the sky as a loud “CRACK” 
resonates through the countryside. 
You begin to think you may be in over 
your head.
Random Encounters
1 - 2  4x Swarm of Ravens 
3 - 4  4x Swarm of Rats 
5 - 6  4x Swarm of Insects
The players wander through the corn 
maze a bit and once they have faced off 
6 “random: encounters, one EVERY 
OTHER turn, the secret entrance 
reveals itself as marked on the map 
below.  
Once the players find the entrance, the 
players will face of the Pumpkin King 
and his Pump-kids, little demon spawn 
that are the reincarnated souls of his 
victims. They can be set anywhere on 
the map in the appropriate area. 
The Statistics for the Swarms can be 
found in the Monster Manual or the 
SRD located at www.playdnd.com or at 
dndbeyond.com. 
Statistics for the Great Pumpkin and the 
Pump-kids are listed on the next page. 
Insect, Raven and Rat Swarms are 
found in the Monster Manual or 5e SRD

The Great Pumpkin
The Great Pumpkin Demonic Construct, Chaotic Evil 
Armor Class 13 
Hit Points 88 (12d12 +12) 
Speed 30 ft 
  STR      DEX       CON        INT        WIS        CHA 
11 (+0)   13 (+1)   11(+0)    14 (+2).  13 (+1)    13 (+1) 
Saving Throws: Dex +1, Cha +1 
Damage Vulnerabilities: Fire 
Damage Resistances: bludgeoning, piercing and 
slashing from non magical attacks 
Damage Immunities: poison 
Condition Immunities: Charmed, exhaustion, 
frightened, paralyzed, poisoned, unconscious 
Senses: darkvision 60 ft, passive Perception 10 
Languages: Understands common and abyssal but 
cant speak 
Challenge: 2 (450 XP) 
False Appearance: While the Great Pumpkin is 
motionless it is indistinguishable from an actual 
scarecrow  
ACTIONS                                                                         
Multi Attack: The Scarecrow makes 2 claw attacks 
Claws: Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft, 
one creature. 7 (2d4+3) If the target is a creature it 
must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or be 
frightened until the end of the Great Pumpkin’s next 
turn. 
Terrifying Glare: The Great Pumpkin targets one 
creature it can see within 30 feet of it. If the target can 
see the Great Pumpkin, the target must succeed on a 
DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or be magically 
frightened until the end of the Great Pumpkin's next 
turn. The Frightened market is paralyzed. 
Raise Pumpkids: Every other turn the great pumpkin 
can raise a pumpkid on a Charisma roll of 15 up to a 
maximum of 6.
The Great Pumpkin is essentially a 
Scarecrow but where other constructs of this 
type are typically infused with the spirit of 
some malevolent person, the pumpkin is 
infused with that of demon! He has taken 
many shapes throughout his long history, his 
two favorite are either that of a Scarecrow or 
that of a tangle mass of vines and pumpkins 
that has a humanoid shape 
(www.reapermini.com has a FANTASTIC 
model of this). Needless to say, he loves to 
claim the souls of children and making little 
hellspawn of undead “Pumpkids”. Small child 
like creatures with the head of a Jack-O-
Lantern. It is a gruesome practice but one 
that given the great pumpkin his powers over 
the centuries and allowed him to sustain his 
life force for centuries

Pumpkids
Pumpkids Small Demonic Undead 
Armor Class 13 
Hit Points 13 (2d8+4) 
Speed 30 ft 
  STR       DEX       CON        INT       WIS     CHA 
10 (+0)   14(+2)   11(+0)      6 (-2)     8 (-1)    5 (-3) 
Damage Vulnerabilities: Bludgeoning 
Damage Resistances: piercing and slashing from 
non magical attacks 
Damage Immunities: poison 
Condition Immunities: exhaustion, frightened, 
poisoned 
Senses: darkvision 60 ft, passive perception 0 
Languages: Understands common but cant speak 
Challenge: 1/4 (50 XP) 
ACTIONS                                                                         
Claws: Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft, 
one creature. 3 (1d4+1)  
Pitiful Gaze: So heartbreaking is the fate of the 
children that on a Wisdom DC 15 check they cast a 
pitiful gaze on one creature. That creature is so 
overcome with sorrow that it is paralyzed for one turn 
The Pumpkids are a very sad creation of 
the children the Great Pumpkin has slain. 
While he feeds on their spirits, the 
tortured souls of his tiny victims are 
placed in what is left of their desiccated 
bodies. As a reflection of their master, the 
Pumpkids heads twist and contort until 
they resemble the leering faces of Jack O’ 
Lanterns. 
These tormented little souls are vicious 
and attack the living on site, unless told 
otherwise by their twisted master.  
Occasionally, he uses these creations on 
All Hallows Eve to lure even more 
youthful victims to their eternal 
damnation. 
Such is the evil of the “Great” Pumpkin.
