the route of the Long Road, depending

that safety has been disturbed by the
two hill giants and their ambush site.
RANDOM ENCOUNTERS
When the party travels the Long Road, they
could potentially encounter a lot of things, as
the arrival of the hill giants has stirred up
some trouble in the area. These encounters
can happen as often as you like, though you
should keep in mind that using too many of
them can slow the story down signiﬁcantly.
Consider checking for a random encounter
twice per adventuring day, once during the day
and once during a long rest. If you do, roll a
d20. If the result is 18 or higher, a random
encounter occurs.
The encounters presented below build upon
those presented in Chapter 3 of Storm King’s
Thunder (SKT p.68). Feel free to mix and
match between the encounters listed there and
those below.

Adjusting the Encounters
Not all random encounters with monsters need to be
resolved through combat. The random encounters
presented here do not need to be adjusted for parties
with diﬀerent average party levels.
Of course, you are still free to do so by adjusting the
type or number of monsters that the characters
encounter.

BANDITS
The characters encounter a gang of bandits.
The gang consists of a bandit captain
(MM p.344) and 1d6 + 3 bandits (MM p.343),
all at half of their maximum hit points.
Originally, the gang had more members, but
they were attacked by Rug and Zog (see
“Veratrem’s Tribal Siege” for more information)
and suffered heavy losses.
The gang confronts the party with the
captain promising not to attack the party in
exchange for a toll (no less than 100 gp worth
of treasure). A DC 10 Wisdom (Insight) check
reveals that the captain is blufﬁng and does
not feel conﬁdent in their chances.
Alternatively, a DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)
check reveals that the bandits don’t look to be
in ﬁghting shape. If the characters pay up, the
captain bids them a safe journey before
departing peacefully. If they don’t and a ﬁght
breaks out, the captain and bandits attempt to
ﬂee after 1 round.
If the characters capture any of the bandits
or the captain, they will attempt to trade
information about the hill giant settlement for
their freedom.

CLOUD CASTLE
Above in the skies, the characters spot a giant
castle.
If the characters have encountered Zephyros
(SKT p.33) in Chapter 1 of Storm King’s
Thunder, it is his castle and they can recognize
it without effort. If the characters have some
way to signal to Zephyros, stairs made of
clouds begin to form underneath the castle
and descend toward them. Zephyros is friendly
and can offer advice, but is not traveling in the
direction that the characters wish to go and
does not offer to take them. He sticks to the
warning he received from the planar entities he
spoke with using the contact other plane spell.
In case the characters haven’t met Zephyros,
the castle poses no threat and ignores them as
it slowly drifts away from them.
FIRE GIANT PITS
The characters come across a site near some
low hills where ﬁre giants have recovered a
fragment of the Vonindod. There is no trace of
the giants, but near the two 30-foot-wide,
20-foot-deep craters are two toppled statues of
dwarves.
The ﬁrst is depicted with a large shield, while
the second is holding a warhammer.
A DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check
reveals religious symbols, indicating that this
might have been a site of special spiritual
importance.
GOBLINS
A group of 1 goblin boss (MM p.166) with 2d4
goblins (MM p.166) attempt to surprise the
characters.
It has been hard for them to ﬁnd food in the
area since the arrival of the hill giants and
they are starving. They ﬁght till the death.
MANTICORES
A group of 1d3 manticores come down from
the sky to attack the party.
SCARED TRADER
The party encounters a scared trader running
their way. The trader, Kogren Visk (LG male
Tethyrian human), is in shock and the
characters will have to ﬁnd a way to calm him
down before they can get some answers out of
him.
When they do, the Kogren tells them he was
attacked by hill giants, who took all of his
wares and his horse. Kogren is too shaken to
remember much about the encounter in detail,
but can describe the general location of the hill
giant settlement.
WOODLAND CREATURES
Rounding a corner in the road, the characters
come upon a place when a pristine forest
stream runs closely to the path.
Around the stream are 2d6 elk, 1d10
badgers, 3d12 frogs, 1d4 tressym and other
small woodland creatures. The beasts aren’t
hostile and ﬂee if attacked.
