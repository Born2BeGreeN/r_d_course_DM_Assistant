Waterdeep
Encounter
Book
Wyrm’s
Birthday
Gift
By
Al
Stover

Synopsis
Residents
and
business
owners
of
Trollskull
Alley
are
celebrating
the
birthday
of
Rishaal
The
Page-Turner
(dragonborn,
he/him),
owner
of
Book
Wyrm’s
Treasure.
When
the
party
arrives
and
sees
all
of
the
fanfare,
they
are
greeted
by
Trollskull
Alley
investigator
Vincent
Trench
(he/him).
The
PI
explains
that
Rishaal
doesn’t
usually
like
celebrating
his
birthday,
yet
Vincent
and
the
other
alley
residents
wanted
to
make
this
year
special
for
the
dragonborn.
Since
Rishaal
owns
a
bookstore
and
loves
books,
Vincent
tasks
the
party
with
gathering
stories
for
an
anthology
they’ll
give
to
Rishaal
as
a
present.
This
encounter
can
be
run
for
any
character
level.
The
party
size
can
range
from
1-5
characters.
Setting
The
encounter
takes
place
in
Waterdeep’s
Trollskull
Alley.
Dungeon
Masters
can
put
this
encounter
into
their
“Waterdeep:
Dragon
Heist”
campaign,
or
any
adventure
set
in
the
City
of
Splendors.
If
the
characters
are
from
“Waterdeep:
Dragon
Heist
''
campaign
and
already
own
Trollskull
Manor,
they’re
likely
familiar
with
their
alley
neighbors,
including
Rishaal.
If
the
party
doesn’t
own
Trollskull
Manor,
or
hasn’t
been
to
Trollskull
Alley,
they
could
be
just
passing
through
and
came
across
this
elaborate
party.
Encounter
Start
The
encounter
begins
when
the
party
enters
Trollskull
Alley.
The
Dungeon
Master
will
read
the
following
description.
Trollskull
Alley
is
filled
with
laughter,
singing,
and
pouring
of
drinks
as
a
party
is
in
full
swing.
Sitting
at
a
large
table
is
an
uncomfortable
dragonborn
who
nibbles
on
a
piece
of
chocolate
cake,
then
smiles
as
an
inebriated
couple
walks
up
to
him
and
shouts
“Happy
Birthday!”
As
you
walk
further
into
the
party,
a
human
wearing
a
trenchcoat
greets
you
with
a
smile
while
holding
a
leather
bound
tome.
Vincent
Trench
greets
the
adventurers
and
hands
one
of
them
a
leatherbound
book.
He
explains
that
it’s
Rishaal’s
birthday.
If
asked
why
Rishaal
doesn’t
look
happy,
Vincent
replies
that
the
dragonborn
doesn’t
like
celebrating
his
birthday.
Recognizing
they’re
adventurers,
Vincent
asks
the
party
to
help
him
in
gathering
stories
and
fill
the
book’s
pages.
Once
they’re
finished,
they’ll
present
the
book
to
Rishaal.
Vincent
adds
that
he’s
already
filled
the
first
several
pages
with
stories
from
himself
and
other
business
owners
in
Trollskull
Alley.
If
the
characters
look
inside,
they’ll
see
the
following
entries
-
Vincent
wrote
about
a
case
where
he
and
the
City
Watch
teamed
up
to
stop
a
group
of
shapechangers
blackmailing
a
noble
family.
-
Embric
(he/him)
and
Avi
(he/him),
of
Steam
and
Steel
penned
an
tale
where
they
discover
schematics
of
elven
chain
armor
in
Ardeep
Forest,
as
well
as
their
attempts
to
create
it.

[PDF_PAGE=3]
-
The
Bent
Nail’s
Talisolvanar
“Tally”
Fellbranch
(he/him),
wrote
about
the
first
shield
his
parents
gave
him.
-
Fala
Lefaliir
(they/them),
of
Corellon’s
Crown,
shared
a
story
about
the
time
she
and
her
friend
Ziraj
accidentally
consumed
a
sleeping
potion.
All
of
the
aforementioned
NPC’s
stat
blocks
can
be
found
in
“Waterdeep:
Dragon
Heist.”
Objective
To
complete
this
encounter,
characters
need
to
collect
stories
from
at
least
six
more
people.
A
character
can
use
their
past
adventure,
experience
or
their
backstory
as
one
of
the
entries.
Characters
can
also
mingle
with
party
guests
to
gather
tales.
There
are
ten
guests
they
can
talk
to.
Anyone
attempting
to
collect
a
story
from
a
guest
will
need
to
succeed
a
DC
12
Charisma
check.
Most
of
these
stories
from
party
guests
can
be
ad
libbed
by
the
Dungeon
Master.
Of
course,
there’s
always
the
option
to
have
a
player
come
up
with
a
guest’s
story.
Characters
can
speak
to
the
following
guests:
1.
Eban
Lorlei
(human,
she/they)
is
a
local
dog
walker
who
shares
the
story
about
her
first
animal
foster,
a
puppy
named
Wanda.
2.
Teeleef
Bramblescar
(halfling,
she/her)
is
a
member
of
the
Dungsweeper’s
Guild
and
writes
about
the
nastiest
mess
she’s
cleaned
up
in
the
North
Ward.
3.
The
cleric
Obaya
Uday
(human,
she/her),
writes
about
her
most
recent
expedition
into
Undermountain.
4.
Vulcara
Ripsheef
(half-elf,
she/her)
is
a
blacksmith
from
Icewind
Dale,
who
boasts
about
the
time
she
wrestled
a
yeti.
5.
Octo
Brewfist
(half-orc,
he/him)
recently
started
brewing
his
own
beer.
He
shares
a
tale
about
working
his
first
shift
in
Shadowdale’s
Old
Skull
Inn.
6.
Roland
Lhal
(aasimar,
he/him)
is
the
Waterdeep
Parks
and
Recreation
Guest
Services
Coordinator.
He
writes
about
the
time
he
and
his
friends
beat
up
Danilo
Thann.
7.
Flint
(dragonborn,
he/him)
is
the
Waterdeep
Parks
and
Recreation
Deputy
Director.
He
writes
about
the
first
painting
class
he
supervised,
which
ended
in
disaster.
8.
Delnar
Grimaxe
(orc,
he/him)
is
a
chieftain
who
reminisces
about
his
first
hunt
in
the
mountains.
9.
Waterdeep
resident
Floon
Blagmaar
(human,
he/him),
shares
a
story
about
him
drinking
with
Renaer
Neverember.
10.
Mirt
(human,
he/him),
a
longtime
Waterdeep
noble,
shares
a
story
of
adventuring
in
Undermountain.
Another
way
characters
can
fill
the
book’s
pages
is
to
pen
down
historical
facts.
Anyone
attempting
to
do
this
will
need
to
make
a
History
(Intelligence)
check
about
Waterdeep
or
another
area
of
Faerun.
They
can
also
utilize
any
spells
that
can
help
them
in
this
skill
check.

[PDF_PAGE=4]
What
the
character
is
able
to
remember
will
depend
on
their
roll.
1-10
-
They
know
basic
knowledge
about
the
story
they’re
trying
to
recall
11-15
-
They
know
great
detail
about
the
story.
16
or
higher
-
They
know
the
exact
details
of
the
story
and
can
recite
it
as
if
they
were
there
themselves.
Conclusion
Once
all
of
the
stories
are
collected,
the
characters
can
present
the
book
to
Rishaal.
The
dragonborn
accepts
it
with
a
genuine
smile,
thanking
them
for
a
wonderful
present.
He
then
raises
a
toast
and
says
how
grateful
and
thankful
he
is
for
being
among
such
great
friends
and
neighbors.
Characters
can
also
go
around
and
try
some
chocolate
cake
and
ale.
If
the
characters
ask
why
Rishaal
doesn’t
like
celebrating
his
birthday,
he
explains
the
sour
feelings
stemming
from
an
incident
during
his
childhood.
He
once
took
notes
for
a
famous
adventuring
guild.
In
return,
the
guild
promised
to
attend
Rishaal’s
birthday
party.
They
never
showed.
Once
he
finishes
his
tale,
Rishaal
says
that
the
efforts
of
the
characters
and
his
friends
made
him
realize
that
you
can’t
let
one
bad
birthday
ruin
future
celebrations.
Once
again
he
thanks
the
characters
for
making
his
birthday
fun
again
and
he
looks
forward
to
next
year’s
celebration.

encounter

inspiration
from
a

sources.

end

Falls”
episode
“Blendin’s
Game.”
